Build a website called "Selfbeat" with the 
tagline "Where AI meets its match — itself."

Users type any question and get answers from 
multiple AI models simultaneously, then watch 
those AI models critically evaluate their own 
answers against each other.

CORE FEATURE — THREE ROUNDS:

Round 1 — All AIs Answer:
Send the user's question simultaneously to 
ChatGPT (OpenAI API), Claude (Anthropic API), 
Gemini (Google API), and DeepSeek API.
Display all four answers side by side in 
clean cards. Each card shows the AI name 
and its logo color.

Round 2 — Self Criticism (The Selfbeat Round):
Send each AI its own answer plus all other 
AI answers and ask it to critically evaluate 
its own response. Each AI must:
- Acknowledge what it got right
- Admit what it missed or got wrong
- Identify which other AI answered better 
  and why
- Give itself an honest score out of 10

Display each AI's self-criticism below 
its original answer in a different colored 
section labeled "Selfbeat Analysis"

Round 3 — The Verdict:
Analyze all answers and self-criticisms 
to produce:
- Accuracy score for each AI out of 10
- Self-awareness score for each AI out of 10
- Best answer for this question with reason
- Clearest answer for general public
- Key points all AIs agreed on
- Key points where AIs disagreed
- Overall winner with full explanation
